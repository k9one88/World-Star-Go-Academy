OUR PHILOSOPHY

Why Learn Go (Weiqi)? We Focus on Lifelong Growth

No rigid rote memorization. With patient and encouraging guidance, every lesson is a training ground for critical thinking and character.

💡

Spark Logical Thinking, Not Rote Memory

We don't train robotic memorizers. Through guided questions and scenario breakdowns, we teach students to observe the whole board, weigh trade-offs, and reason independently.

🌱

Build Adversity Quotient (AQ) & Resilience

Many children fear losing and get frustrated easily when behind. Through supportive post-game reviews, we guide students to embrace mistakes calmly and turn setbacks into the courage to try again.

🤝

Warm & Engaging Small-Group Classes

Teaching since 2011, we understand every child's attention span. Interactive challenges keep active kids engaged and help them fall in love with deep focus.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from parents and new students:

Q1: My child is a complete beginner and very active. Will they be able to sit still?
Absolutely! For beginners, we avoid dry lectures and instead use interactive challenges, storytelling, and fast-paced capturing games to naturally stretch their attention span.
Q2: My child hates losing and gets upset easily when defeated. Can Go help?
That is precisely why Go is so valuable. Cultivating Adversity Quotient (AQ) is at the heart of our teaching. We gently guide children to shift focus from "I lost" to "What move could work better next time?" in a safe, encouraging environment.
Q3: What is the best age to start? Can adults with zero experience learn too?
Children can start from age 5 and above. And for adults, any age is a great age to start! Our adult sessions break down concepts using clear, practical logic so you can enjoy real games right from Lesson 1.
Q4: Can we book a trial class before committing to a full term?
Yes! We warmly welcome parents and children to experience our classroom atmosphere first. Simply fill out the 30-second booking form above to schedule your slot.
